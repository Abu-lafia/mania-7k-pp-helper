Sources & calculations

Player pool

Global 7K ranks from osu!. PP boundaries are inclusive and checked against exact 7K profile pp. PP searches continue beyond rank 3,000, up to the public leaderboard's 10,000-player limit.

Dan levels

Only Mania Tracker's 7K regular and LN records. Rating is the site's numerical dan rating. Suffixes are retained in player lists and merged for histograms. Missing records are reported separately.

Best performances

Top 50 osu!mania best performances per player, including stable and lazer. Only native 7K mania maps enter the pool; standard-mode converts are excluded. Each beatmap ID has up to three entries: HT, NM and DT/NC. Other mods do not exclude scores. Frequency counts distinct players.

PP ceiling

rosu-pp-js 4.0.1: 100% perfect, full combo, osu!stable calculation. HT is 0.75x, NM is 1x, DT/NC is 1.5x. Other mods are ignored for the normalized calculation. This is a calculated ceiling, not the player's achieved pp.

Local storage

Rankings and pp are cached for one hour; best performances and tracker records for six hours. Beatmap calculations are cached by checksum and engine version. A bounded memory cache sits in front of local SQLite. Refresh source data bypasses the timed caches. Optional local Redis is configured in config.json and is disabled by default.

Downloads

All download links exclude video. Individual osu! downloads may require signing in. Bulk downloads use SayoBot and save one .osz per beatmapset in the local downloads folder.

This is an independent community tool, not affiliated with osu! or Mania Tracker.
